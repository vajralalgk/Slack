## :page_facing_up: Executive Summary

> **:bulb: Strategic Vision**
> The Enterprise Cloud Transformation Platform (ECTP) is an organization-wide initiative to modernize Higher Education IT infrastructure through cloud migration, IT service management integration, Higher Education system modernization, and enterprise DevOps automation.

## :calendar: Timeline & Roadmap

> **:rocket: Phased Delivery Approach**
> ECTP follows a four-phase delivery model, each building upon the previous phase. This approach reduces risk, enables iterative feedback, and ensures foundational capabilities are established before advanced features are deployed.
